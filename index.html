Welcome to BBSC

A Design Build Firm
Serving South Carolina

BBSC’s extensive expertise in design and installation of wood doors and windows, hardwood flooring, cabinetry and millwork, combined with our ability to provide comprehensive and quality construction management, as well as value engineering, is what separates us from our competition.

We started in California. BBSC built its name there over three decades — landmark hotels, schools, city buildings and custom homes — and we have now brought that team and those standards to South Carolina, serving Columbia, Lexington and the surrounding Midlands. Read our story

With BBSC’s Principals and Project Managers, we offer over 30 years of expertise in the building industry. BBSC provides a variety of services and products for our clients in commercial and residential markets.

  • Design, manufacturing and installation handled under one roof

What We Do

Complete Construction Services & Supplies

Large or small, commercial or residential — our satisfied clientele come from all areas. Our work is guaranteed and our ability to accommodate all your needs sets us apart from our competitors.

New Construction

Ground-up custom homes, multi-family dwellings and commercial buildings delivered under a single design build contract.

Renovation

Full remodels and phased renovations for occupied homes, hotels and public buildings — on schedule and on budget.

Doors and Windows

Wood, vinyl and clad windows plus exterior and interior doors — custom designed, manufactured and installed by our own crews.

Hardwood Flooring

Supply and installation of hardwood and vinyl floors, including sanding, custom staining and on-site finishing.

Thermal Control

Infrared inspections that reveal heat loss, moisture intrusion, hidden leaks and electrical faults before they turn into costly repairs. More on infrared inspections.

Architectural Details

Corbels, columns, coffered ceilings, wainscot and trim packages that give a building its character.

Interior Design

Our design studio helps you visualize and select furniture, plumbing, lighting and fine accessories for the whole project.

Consulting

Pre-construction advice on design intent, material selection, architectural themes and budget before the first invoice.
