Thermal Imaging

See Beyond the
Normally Visible

We begin with a thorough examination inspecting the home’s walls, plumbing, wiring, ceilings and floors. When differences in temperature show up on our thermal imaging system we are able to gather information from the results.

This in turn allows us to assess problems with water damage, energy loss, electrical problems, roof and pipe leaks, mold growth, infestations and structural defects — before they turn into a repair bill.
